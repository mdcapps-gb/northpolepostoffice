North Pole Post Office

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Your family’s letters belong to your family.
North Pole Post Office stores letters, wish lists and photos locally. The app does not upload them to the developer, require an account, show adverts or include third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored in the app

To provide its features, North Pole Post Office saves information you choose to enter or add, including:

  • Children’s names, chosen profile pictures and colours.
  • Wish lists, gift photos and optional notes about where a gift was seen.
  • Letter drafts, posted letters, Santa’s replies, personal details added to replies and reply extras.
  • Delivery times, whether replies have been opened and your app preferences.

This content is stored on your device and is not sent to the developer. We do not operate a server that receives your family’s letters, wish lists or photos.

No advertising or tracking

The app does not use advertising networks, third-party analytics tools or tracking SDKs. It does not collect your location or send usage information or advertising identifiers to the developer.

Photos and camera

You can choose a photo or use the camera to add a gift picture. Camera access is optional and requested when you choose to take a photo. Photos saved by the app are resized and re-encoded without the original location metadata. Deleting a saved gift photo from the app does not delete its original from your Photos library.

Grown-up lock

The grown-up PIN is stored as a salted hash in the device’s Keychain, rather than as readable PIN digits. The app uses Apple’s authentication system for optional Face ID or Touch ID and device authentication. It does not receive or store your facial data, fingerprints or device passcode.

Optional writing assistance

On supported devices, “Help me write” uses Apple’s on-device language model to suggest a reply. The child’s name, wishes, letter and any personal detail you provide are processed on the device for this feature. The app does not send them to the developer or an external AI service. A grown-up can review and edit the suggested reply before arranging delivery.

Notifications

With permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device to announce that a reply has arrived. It does not use a developer-operated push notification service. You can turn notifications off in the app or your device settings.

Printing and sharing

A grown-up can choose to create a PDF and print it, save it or share it using the device’s share sheet. Gift photos are included only when the grown-up turns on that option. If you share or save a keepsake through another app or service, that service handles the shared information under its own privacy practices. The app does not automatically share keepsakes.

Device backups

The app does not provide its own cloud syncing or online storage. Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in an iCloud or computer backup managed by Apple. Those backups are separate from the app and are controlled through your device and Apple services. See Apple’s information about iCloud Backup.

Children’s privacy

North Pole Post Office is designed for families to use together. A grown-up sets up children’s profiles and controls Santa’s replies, sharing and app settings. Children do not need an online account. The app does not transmit children’s profiles, photos or letters to the developer. Use only the details you are comfortable storing on your device.

Deleting information

A grown-up can delete an individual child and their associated content in the grown-ups area. To remove all children, letters, replies and saved gift photos from the app, choose Grown-ups → Settings & privacy → Erase all letters and children. The grown-up PIN is kept separately in the Keychain. Keychain information may remain after uninstalling the app.

Deleting content in the app does not remove PDFs you have exported, original photos in your Photos library, copies you have shared or copies in existing device backups. Manage those separately in the relevant app or service.

Contacting support

If you email mdcprice1@gmail.com, we receive your email address and anything you include in your message or attachments. We use this correspondence to respond to and resolve your request. Email is handled through our email provider, Gmail. Please do not send children’s private letters or photos, your grown-up PIN or your device passcode. You can contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable legal retention requirements.

This website

These support and privacy pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies. They are intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub handles requests to the website under its own privacy practices. This is separate from data stored locally in the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s privacy practices change, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact
