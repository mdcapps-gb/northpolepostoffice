Getting started

  1. Open Grown-ups on the writing desk, create a grown-up PIN and add a child. You can add more children, each with their own letters, wish list and mailbox.
  2. Help your child add wishes, write to Santa, choose a stamp and post their letter.
  3. Return to Grown-ups → Write Santa’s replies to prepare a reply and choose when it arrives.
  4. When it arrives, your child can open it in their Magic mailbox.

Frequently asked questions

Who writes Santa’s replies?

A grown-up prepares each reply in the protected grown-ups area. Write your own words or start with a template, add personal touches, preview the letter and choose its arrival time. Replies are not sent by an external Santa service.

Why hasn’t a reply arrived?

Check Grown-ups → Write Santa’s replies to make sure a reply has been prepared and check its scheduled arrival time. A child’s posted letter does not receive a reply until a grown-up arranges one. Notifications are optional; replies can still be opened in the app if notifications are off.

Can I change a reply?

You can edit or take back a reply until your child opens it. Once opened, it is kept as received.

I’ve forgotten my grown-up PIN

Choose Forgotten your PIN? on the grown-up lock screen and follow the device authentication prompts to set a new PIN. Five incorrect PIN attempts pause entry for one minute.

Can I use Face ID or Touch ID?

On a supported device, enable it during setup or in Grown-ups → Settings & privacy. Only enable biometric unlocking if your children’s faces or fingerprints are not enrolled on the device.

How do I turn on arrival notifications?

Enable Notify me when Santa’s reply arrives in Grown-ups → Settings & privacy. If notifications are blocked, allow them for NP Post Office in your device’s Settings app. Focus and notification settings can affect when alerts appear.

Why can’t I hear any sounds?

Turn on Gentle sounds in Settings & privacy, check your device volume and turn off Silent Mode. The app’s sounds are intentionally gentle.

How do I print or save a keepsake?

Open Grown-ups → Print & share keepsakes, choose a child and a wish list, letter or reply, then preview it. Choose Share or save as PDF, or Print. Gift photos are excluded unless you turn on Include gift photos. Printing requires a compatible printer.

Why don’t I see “Help me write”?

Writing assistance is available only when Apple’s on-device language model is available on a compatible device running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later with Apple Intelligence enabled. You can always write your own reply or use a template.

Do letters sync between devices?

No. The app does not provide account-based syncing or a cloud letter service. Letters and wish lists are saved locally. Depending on your settings, Apple’s device backups may include app data. Save important letters as PDF keepsakes before deleting the app or changing devices.

How do I remove a child or erase the app’s content?

In Grown-ups, select a child and choose the delete option to remove their profile, letters, replies and saved gift photos. To remove all children and their content, choose Settings & privacy → Erase all letters and children. This cannot be undone and keeps your grown-up PIN. Export keepsakes first if you want to keep them.

Something still isn’t working

Close and reopen the app, check for an update, then contact the support email above if the problem continues. Please contact us before deleting and reinstalling the app, as that can remove locally stored content.
